## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum-ii-input-array-is-sorted/description/

### Approach

We use two pointer to check every possible pair of numbers in the array. If the sum of two numbers is equal to the target, we return their indexes+1.

### Complexity

* Time: O(n)
* Space: O(1)

### Notes

The array is sorted and index starts from 0. We use two pointer(start and end pointer) so that we check each pair only once.
